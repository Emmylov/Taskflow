---
name: TaskFlow portfolio media
description: Product rule for service portfolio examples and future media replacement.
---

Service pages can use designed representative previews to communicate output formats before real portfolio media is available. They must be labeled as representative examples, not presented as completed client work. When approved images or videos are supplied, replace the preview art without changing the service-page gallery structure.

**Why:** The storefront needs to communicate quality now without inventing client results or implying that placeholder visuals are real work.

**How to apply:** Keep three examples per service in the existing gallery. Accept real images, short videos, social posts, brand boards, workflow diagrams, and landing-page screenshots as replacements. Feature selected approved examples on the homepage only after the files are supplied.
